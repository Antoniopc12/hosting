Hi!, I'm Antonio Peña Castillo

Hi, I'm Antonio Peña Castillo, and I'm an electronic systems engineer from Málaga, Spain. I graduated in Electronic Systems from the University of Málaga and have developed strong expertise in embedded systems, programming, and IoT.

One of my proudest achievements was earning third place in the third edition of the Dinapsis Hackathon, where my team and I developed an innovative buoy for water quality monitoring. I have also worked on projects like a laser tag system and am currently developing a macropad.

I am passionate about using technology to solve real-world challenges and have developed a keen ability to create practical solutions that make a difference. In my free time, I enjoy staying active through sports, especially handball and beach handball, which I have been playing since I was 10. I also enjoy playing padel, soccer, climbing, and exploring other sports.

Currently, I work as an R&D Engineer at Airzone, where I continue to grow both professionally and personally. My perseverance and sometimes stubborn determination, along with my problem-solving skills, set me apart in my field.

I am currently focused on expanding my skills and knowledge, always striving to achieve new milestones in my career and personal life. I am eager to tackle new challenges and bring innovative solutions to the ever-evolving world of technology.
